# Market notes

Numbers gathered on 5 September 2026 while shaping the product. Each carries its source type so it can be re-checked.

## The trend

- "Winter arc" originates in anime training-arc language, first seen as a fitness phrase in 2022, mainstream on TikTok in autumn 2024, rebranded as the four-month "Great Lock In" in 2025, added to Merriam-Webster's slang entries in 2026.
- Roughly half a million TikTok posts under the hashtag by late 2024. The 2024 meme "winter arc is not a double XP event" mocks the drop-off after two days.
- The existing "winter arc" apps are iOS-only habit trackers with no proof, no squad, no stakes and no published user numbers.

## Why squads and stakes

- Around 80 percent of New Year resolutions are abandoned by the second week of January, from Strava activity data.
- Health and fitness apps retain 3 to 4 percent of installs at day 30.
- Commitment contracts roughly tripled success in stickK's data; a referee lifts success to 61 percent, self-reported.
- A randomized trial of weight-loss incentives found group-based incentives beat individual ones by about seven pounds over 24 weeks, with a pot split among a group of five who met goals.
- One third of Duolingo's daily actives keep a friend streak.
- Forfeit reports a 94 percent success rate across 75,000 goals with stakes plus photo proof, self-reported.

## Why now

- Apple Workout Buddy, Strava Athlete Intelligence and Whoop Coach shipped or expanded between late 2025 and mid-2026. AI coaching is the floor, not the differentiator.
- Strava passed 195 million users and a 500 million dollar run rate; running clubs grew 3.5 times in a year; over half of Strava's 16 to 26 year olds plan to use it more in 2026.
- Hyrox expects 1.5 million participants in 2026. Ironman first-timers grew 12 percent in North America in 2025, with women under 30 up 36 percent.
- Cal AI reached a 50 million dollar run rate with seven employees on creator marketing: 281 founder videos before ads, 150 creators on retainer posting four times a month, then paid, then affiliates.

## Constraints that shaped the architecture

- Strava's API terms forbid displaying a member's data to others and forbid AI use.
- Garmin's direct developer onboarding was reported paused in 2026; Terra provides Garmin through its own partnership at 399 dollars a month on annual.
- Hard paywalls convert installs at 10.7 percent median versus 2.1 percent for freemium; annual plans carry 60.6 percent of health and fitness subscription revenue.
- Forfeit and Beeminder charge stakes through Stripe outside in-app purchase; stickK sends forfeits to charity or anti-charity.
