# 0010. Mind and money lines are squad-witnessed, not model-verified

Date: 2026-09-05. Status: accepted for season one.

## Context

The verifier and its rubric are built for sport proofs: a place, a person, a sport. Habit lines verify by attestation, a photo or an artifact, and routing them through the dual-cam ritual produced asks the member could not answer. Habits are capped at two per contract so they never carry most of the stake.

## Decision

`attest_today` stamps a habit line Bronze from the member's word (attest) or one photo from the in-app camera (photo, artifact), stored in the member's own folder and shown on the Squad Board like any proof. The verify-proof function refuses these lines. The day mark is B, the same mark a vouched proof gets, because the trust model is the same: people who know you, looking.

## Consequences

Cheating on a habit line is possible and visible, which is the social contract of a small squad. No model cost for habits. When a habit pack earns its own rubric, the RPC and the screen stay and the model call is added behind them.
