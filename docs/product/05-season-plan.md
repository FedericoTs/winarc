# Season one plan

Today is 5 September 2026. The Great Lock In started on 1 September. Season one opens 1 October and squads lock on 7 October.

## Validation, 5 to 14 September

- Landing page with two variants, "start solo" versus "find your squad". Measure signup completion and day-seven proof rate.
- Concierge cohort in a chat group from 15 September: manual dual-cam proofs, squads of five, a virtual pot. Pass if the daily proof rate stays above 70 percent in week two.
- One hundred creator messages with a revenue-share offer. Target five founding creators whose codes open their own squads.
- Build the verification eval set: 500 proofs, 100 adversarial. Ship when false accepts are under 5 percent and false rejects under 10 percent.

## Build, 15 to 30 September

Must ship by 1 October: contract and poster, squads with codes and a hand-matched draft, dual-cam proof with verification, HealthKit auto-verify, Squad Board, Sunday settlement on the ledger, share cards, push notifications.

TestFlight by 22 September. App Store submission by 24 September. Creator posts on 29 September.

## Season, 1 October to 31 December

- Weekly ship cadence driven by retention, not roadmap.
- Three viral moments: Day 30, Day 60 and the finale.
- By Day 30: widget and Live Activity, episode cards, Android via Health Connect.
- By Day 60: Terra connector, the first sport pack, video episodes, the finale fund if counsel clears it.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Day 0 poster share rate | 15 percent of signed contracts |
| Day-30 retention | 25 percent or better against a category baseline of 3 to 4 |
| 90-day completion, squads with stakes | 40 percent or better |
| Invites per new member | 1.2 or better |
| Week-three intervention lift versus holdout | 20 percent fewer drop-offs |
| Fraud rate on verified proofs | under 2 percent |

## Not this season

Stripe or any payment for stakes. Charity beneficiaries. A feed. Coaching. A second squad for the same contract. Crews.
