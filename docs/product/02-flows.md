# Flows

The thirteen moments, in the order a member meets them. The prototype in `docs/prototype/season-one.html` is the reference for each screen; this page records what happens and why.

## 1. Countdown

The first screen is a countdown to 1 October, not a feature tour. One number, one button. No sign-in before the contract exists.

## 2. Sports

Twenty-two sport chips plus a custom one, pick one to three. Sport is identity: it sets the proof scene, the contract templates and who you are drafted with. A custom sport verifies by photo and by Health when the watch logs it under any workout type.

## 3. Contract

Lines come from sport templates with a weekly frequency and default training days. The weigh-in is weekly, private and optional. Ten mind and money presets or your own with a verification method, capped at two. Frequencies and days are editable until squads lock on 7 October.

## 4. Squad and terms

Three doors. Found: name, size two to eight, stake tier, pot rule, then one code with spots left and the lock date. Join: enter a code, inherit the terms, no settings. Draft: matched by sport, timezone and frequency within a day, by hand in season one.

## 5. Sign

The contract renders as a poster with the squad code, spots left and the lock date. Sign on glass, then share. Every Day 0 post becomes an invite with a deadline.

## 6. Today

What is due, the deadline, the squad's status, and the rescue line. "Can't train?" opens the three honest options: use a sick day, ask the squad to vouch, take the miss. Push at 20:00 only if the proof is missing; the app asks for notification permission once, right after the contract is signed, when the reason is obvious. On the weigh-in day Today adds a private row: a Health reading or a typed number, never staked, never on the board, and the only number shown back is the change since the first reading.

## 7. Proof

Rear camera, front camera, one second apart. Verification returns in seconds and ends with a stamp. On an ask: retake first, vouch second, appeal third. Nothing is charged.

Mind and money lines never go through the model. An attest line is one tap, a photo or artifact line is one photo from the in-app camera; both go on the board for the squad to see and stamp Bronze.

## 8. Board

Members by day. Verified in ember, vouched in mint, misses in rose, rest quiet, today pulsing. Glyph plus color on every cell. Tap a cell to see the proof behind it. Squad only.

## 9. Ledger

Sunday at 21:00 local, and on the finale day. Misses become pot entries, hit rate and MVP are named, and the ledger email goes out. Sunday proofs are due by 21:00 because the ledger closes the week.

## 10. Episode

Days 30, 60 and 90 each render an episode from the member's proofs and stats, titled by the model in training-arc language. A card in season one, a video when the card's share rate passes 20 percent.

## 11. Finale

31 December. Certificate, season stats, the pot vote, and the final settlement. The next arc opens 1 January with the same squad and the same code.

## 12. Cards

Six cards: poster, proof stamp, board strip, ledger, episode, certificate. All pass the screenshot test and all carry the join code.

## 13. Lock screen

A Live Activity counts down the proof deadline. A widget shows the day and who has proved. People photograph their lock screens.

## Daily and weekly clocks

- A member's day is their local calendar date. Days open at midnight with a pending mark for every line due that weekday and close at 23:59 with pending marks becoming misses.
- Sundays and the finale day close at 21:00 when the ledger runs.
- Sick days write a rest entry for the day and tell the squad. Vouches need two squadmates within 24 hours and stamp Bronze.
